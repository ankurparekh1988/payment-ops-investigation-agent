# Settlement closure days policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Contoso handles settlement system closure days, when processors don't settle and banks don't move funds, and what merchants are told about them.

The closure dates themselves are kept in the settlement calendar. This policy doesn't list them.

## How closure days affect settlement

### No batches close

Processors don't close settlement batches on a closure day. Payments captured that day join the next settlement day's batch, together with that day's own captures.

### Payouts move to the next settlement day

A payout that would have been made on a closure day is made on the next settlement day instead. We don't make payouts early to get ahead of a closure.

### Consecutive closures

When closure days fall next to a weekend or each other, the delay adds up. Count settlement days, not calendar days, when working out an expected date.

## Telling merchants

### Before a closure

Merchant communications announce upcoming closure days in advance, with the effect on payout dates. Operations doesn't need to contact merchants individually.

### When a merchant asks afterwards

Explain that the payout date moved because of the closure day, and give the date the payout was or will be made. A delay caused only by a closure day is not an incident, and no ticket is raised.

## When a closure day isn't the whole story

If a payout is later than the closure day alone explains, investigate it as a pending settlement. A closure day often hides a second cause, such as a capture that also missed the cut-off.
